SIGNBOARD IMAGE STATUS

The project intentionally contains no placeholder or internet-sourced signboard illustrations.
The signboard image area stays blank and displays “Image not available” until the original
snapshot files referenced by backend/data/signs.csv are supplied.

To add the real images, copy the original snapshot JPG files into:
backend/public/snapshots/

Keep the filenames exactly as listed in the CSV snapshot column. The backend automatically
serves a matching snapshot when it exists. The IITM logo and campus-road background remain
in public/images/.
